Ridge loop

A loop that climbs from the lake to the ridge and returns through the forest.

Length
8.4 km
Elevation gain
410 m
Typical time
3 hours
Surface
Packed earth, with rocky steps near the ridge
Water
A spring at the halfway point
Parking
The lakeside lot, which fills by mid-morning

Route

Leave the lot on the shore path and turn uphill at the first footbridge. The climb is steady for two kilometres, then levels along the ridge, where the lake stays in view on the left.

At the stone marker, take the right fork into the forest. The descent is gentler than the climb and ends at the north end of the lot.
